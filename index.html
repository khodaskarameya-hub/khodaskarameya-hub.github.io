About

I'm a UX researcher and product designer who starts with people, not pixels.

My background in Computer Science taught me how products get built; my MS in Human-Centered Design Technology at Pace University taught me how to make sure they're worth building.

At Fabtech Projects and Engineers I led research for internal platforms: interviewing teams across departments, mapping procurement workflows, redesigning information architecture, and establishing lean design components that shortened delivery by two weeks.

My case studies take on messy, multi-person workflows in healthcare, fintech and research operations, with AI that explains itself where it helps. The thread is the same: frame the problem with evidence, name the risky assumptions, make trade-offs explicit, and plan how to validate.
